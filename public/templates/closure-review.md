## Checklist

<!-- Tick a box only when the record meets it as written. For every box left
     open, write one line under Findings naming the section and what is
     missing. -->

- [ ] The decision header is complete: verdict on both axes in the fixed vocabulary, the Escalation decision with who made it and when, priority with its matrix cell or override, and a bottom line a non-technical reader can follow
- [ ] Every factual claim cites its source: a query and time range, a log record ID, or an artifact hash
- [ ] Confidence is labeled per claim on the shared rubric, not once for the case
- [ ] Negative findings are recorded with what was examined, not just "nothing found"
- [ ] The timeline is in UTC with the date, and each entry has an actor, rationale, and outcome
- [ ] Every open question from the handoff packet is resolved in section 2 or 3, or carried into section 8
- [ ] Every indicator and observable has a disposition (malicious, suspicious, or known-good)
- [ ] Shared and training versions are sanitized, and people are written about neutrally
- [ ] Material AI use is declared, "none" included
- [ ] Benign and authorized closes state their reopen conditions
