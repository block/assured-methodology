# What you have

Across seven chapters, the methodology gave you:

- **A vocabulary.** Seven letters, each answering one question. A shared language that travels across teams and across cases.
- **A structure.** Three phases that group the seven letters into a logical flow. Preparation, investigation, resolution.
- **A discipline.** Each phase has a deliverable. Each deliverable is the input to the next phase. Skipping a phase shows up downstream.
- **A scaffold.** New analysts have a map. Experienced analysts have a checklist. Both find the methodology useful for their own reasons.

# Where to go from here

- **Apply it to your next alert.** The methodology is not a textbook; it is a practice. Use the structure on your next investigation and notice where you would otherwise have skipped a step.
- **Teach it.** The methodology was designed to be taught. Mentoring a newer analyst through their first ASSURED-structured case is one of the fastest ways to refine your own use of it.
- **Contribute.** The methodology lives at [github.com/block/assured-methodology](https://github.com/block/assured-methodology). Feedback, corrections, and contributions are welcome.
