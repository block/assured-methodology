<ChapterExample title="Closing the case durably" scenario="Escalation handed the case to IR. IR contained the intrusion. The triage analyst's Documentation phase produces the record that survives the case.">

<ExampleStepper steps={["Capture the record", "Sanitize for sharing", "Link related artifacts", "Feed detection engineering", "Submit for review"]}>

  <div data-step class="example-step">
    <h3>Step 1. Capture the record</h3>
    <p>The eight-section template is filled, ideally in real time as the investigation ran. Summary, timeline (adversary and analyst columns), entities, evidence chain, Risk verdict, actions taken, open questions, references. The IR responder's findings are linked or summarized as they come in.</p>
  </div>

  <div data-step class="example-step">
    <h3>Step 2. Sanitize for sharing</h3>
    <p>The record needs to be usable by the broader team without exposing sensitive data. PII is masked, regulated content is referenced rather than reproduced, sensitive infrastructure detail is generalized where possible. The version stored in the case-management system stays detailed; the version shared in training stays useful but safe.</p>
  </div>

  <div data-step class="example-step">
    <h3>Step 3. Link related artifacts</h3>
    <p>The record links to: the original alert in the SIEM, the IR ticket in the response system, the compliance notification record, the vendor request thread, the post-mortem document, and the detection-engineering ticket for the rules that need tuning. Every relevant artifact is one click away.</p>
  </div>

  <div data-step class="example-step">
    <h3>Step 4. Feed detection engineering</h3>
    <p>The case surfaced two detection gaps and one false-positive opportunity. The triage analyst writes a short detection-engineering brief: which rules fired (and how usefully), which patterns were missed, and what changes would prevent or sharpen detection next time. The brief becomes a ticket in the detection-engineering queue.</p>
  </div>

  <div data-step class="example-step">
    <h3>Step 5. Submit for review</h3>
    <p>The record is submitted to the SOC manager for closure review. The review is not theater; it is the quality check that ensures the record meets the standard. The reviewer either accepts the record, requests clarification, or flags it as a training case for the team.</p>
  </div>

</ExampleStepper>

</ChapterExample>

<KeyTakeaway>Documentation made the finance-team case durable. The record survives the analyst, the manager, and the moment. Future analysts will learn from it; future audits will rely on it.</KeyTakeaway>
