# Internal tiers

<div class="tier-list">
  <div class="tier-row"><span class="tier-row__label">Tier 1</span><h4>Initial detection and triage</h4><p>Receives alerts. Validates the signal. Closes false positives. Escalates anything that meets criteria or that exceeds Tier 1's mandate. The Alert, Subject, Scope work usually lives here.</p></div>
  <div class="tier-row"><span class="tier-row__label">Tier 2</span><h4>In-depth investigation</h4><p>Picks up escalated cases. Runs Uncover and Risk to completion. Decides whether the case becomes a declared incident. Manages most of the active investigation work.</p></div>
  <div class="tier-row"><span class="tier-row__label">Tier 3</span><h4>Cross-functional coordination</h4><p>For declared incidents. Coordinates with engineering, legal, compliance, communications, and leadership. Often equivalent to the incident commander role for the incident in question.</p></div>
</div>

---

# External paths

<div class="ext-grid">
  <div class="ext"><h4>🧑‍⚖️ Legal counsel</h4><p>Engaged whenever regulatory triggers, customer notifications, or litigation considerations come into play. Often early in the timeline for incidents with regulatory exposure.</p></div>
  <div class="ext"><h4>🏛️ Compliance</h4><p>Engaged when the case touches a regulated framework. Their clock may run independently from the technical investigation.</p></div>
  <div class="ext"><h4>🤝 Vendors</h4><p>When the incident involves a vendor's product or platform. Vendor disclosure may be required by contract.</p></div>
  <div class="ext"><h4>👮 Law enforcement</h4><p>For criminal activity, extortion, or cross-border threats. Engaged through legal counsel, not directly by the SOC.</p></div>
  <div class="ext"><h4>📢 Communications</h4><p>For incidents that may become public. Press, customer, and partner messaging is its own workstream.</p></div>
</div>

<KeyTakeaway>Internal tiers handle the technical work. External paths handle the non-technical consequences. Both are part of the escalation map.</KeyTakeaway>
