Alert through Escalation produced the investigation and the response handoff. Documentation captures the full arc as durable institutional knowledge. The transition is not a new phase of work, it is the formalization of work that has been embedded throughout.
</Callout>

## Documentation is continuous, not final

Rather than being deferred until the end of an investigation, documentation begins early and evolves alongside triage, investigation, and resolution. Critical observations, decisions, and actions are captured in real time. The methodology rejects the pattern of "investigate first, document later" because retrospective documentation loses detail, accuracy, and the reasoning behind decisions at the moment they were made.

## What Escalation hands to Documentation

By the time Escalation has run its course, much of the documentation is already done. The formal Documentation phase is about quality-controlling, structuring, and finalizing what exists.

<div class="handoff-grid">
  <div class="handoff-card">
    <span class="handoff-card__num">01</span>
    <h4>The nine-section packet</h4>
    <p>Case summary, timeline, entities, evidence chain, Risk verdict, containment actions, artifacts, open questions, communication record. Already structured by Escalation.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="handoff-card">
    <span class="handoff-card__num">02</span>
    <h4>Escalation rationale</h4>
    <p>Why the case crossed the criteria threshold. The specific evidence supporting the decision. This becomes part of the audit record.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="handoff-card">
    <span class="handoff-card__num">03</span>
    <h4>Stakeholder engagement record</h4>
    <p>Who was engaged, when, what they were told, what they decided. The communication record that survives the case.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="handoff-card">
    <span class="handoff-card__num">04</span>
    <h4>Open improvement items</h4>
    <p>Detection-engineering gaps, process inefficiencies, tooling limitations identified during the response. Each one becomes a tracked action.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<Reveal summary="What Documentation does with the handoff" variant="info">

Documentation's job is not to start writing. It is to:

- **Verify completeness** against the standards (format, language, timeliness).
- **Apply consistent terminology** across the record.
- **Ensure timestamps and attributions** are present for every action.
- **Translate technical detail** into stakeholder-appropriate summaries.
- **Quality-control before closure** so the artifact stands up to audit, <DefineTerm term="Post-Mortem" display="post-mortem" />, and legal review months later.

The case closes only when Documentation passes its quality bar. The methodology rejects "we will document later" because later usually means never.

</Reveal>

<KeyTakeaway>Escalation moved the case forward. Documentation makes it durable. The transition is not a new task; it is the formalization of work that has been continuous throughout the lifecycle. Documentation is the phase that says "the case is complete," and it does so by quality-controlling what the rest of the methodology has already captured.</KeyTakeaway>
