# B.A.D. Glossary

**B**ad **A**ctor **D**ictionary, a reference for the adversarial vocabulary that shows up in triage. The glossary will be organized by category:

## Sections planned

- **Core adversarial techniques**: foundational behaviors and broad offensive concepts.
- **Threat actor profiles**: named groups and individuals known for malicious cyber activity.
- **Notable campaigns and breaches**: high-impact, widely analyzed intrusions.
- **Malware types**: categories defined by technique, payload, evasion characteristics.
- **Malware families**: named tools and campaign malware tied to specific actors.
- **Exploited vulnerabilities**: CVEs and architectural flaws used in real intrusions.
- **Credential access and abuse**: techniques for stealing, abusing, or manipulating identity data.
- **Execution and injection**: code execution, injection, process manipulation tactics.
- **Command and control infrastructure**: communication mechanisms adversaries rely on.
- **Data theft and exfiltration**: behaviors focused on stealing or leaking data.
- **Evasion and stealth techniques**: methods for blending into normal activity.
- **Initial access techniques**: entry vectors and tactics for gaining a foothold.
