{/* 1. Threat intel */}
<section id="intel" class="cat-section">
  <header class="cat-section__head">
    <span class="cat-section__ico">📡</span>
    <div>
      <h2>Advisories &amp; threat intelligence feeds</h2>
      <p>Sources of curated indicators, campaign reports, and analyst-grade intelligence.</p>
    </div>
  </header>
  <div class="cat-tools">
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>Abuse.ch</h4><p>Community-driven malware infrastructure tracking; free public feeds for malicious IPs, domains, and hashes. Strong on ransomware family tracking.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>AlienVault OTX</h4><p>Collaborative threat-intel exchange aggregating IOCs from a global community, with SIEM/automated integrations.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>Anomali ThreatStream</h4><p>Collects, analyzes, and prioritizes threat data with enrichment and risk scoring; integrates with existing security stacks.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>CISA Advisories</h4><p>U.S. Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency advisories on vulnerabilities and active threats, with mitigation guidance.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>E-ISAC</h4><p>Electricity sector ISAC; threat intel, IR support, and risk analysis tailored to grid operators.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>FBI Flash Reports</h4><p>Rapid high-priority notifications about imminent cyber threats targeting public and private partners.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>FS-ISAC</h4><p>Global financial-sector ISAC providing real-time alerts, incident coordination, and member collaboration.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>GreyNoise</h4><p>Filters internet background-scan noise so SOCs focus on targeted attacks. IP context with malicious vs. benign classification.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>H-ISAC</h4><p>Healthcare-sector ISAC; threat intelligence, incident response coordination, and best practices for clinical environments.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>Intel 471</h4><p>Commercial deep/dark-web monitoring with adversary infrastructure, TTP, and threat-actor behavior intelligence.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>MITRE ATT&amp;CK</h4><p>Global knowledge base of adversary tactics, techniques, and procedures. The shared vocabulary of modern SOCs.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>MS-ISAC</h4><p>Multi-State ISAC for U.S. state, local, tribal, and territorial governments; alerts, IR, and risk management resources.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>NCSC (UK)</h4><p>UK National Cyber Security Centre; guidance, threat intelligence, and IR services across public and private sectors.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>Microsoft Defender Threat Intelligence (MDTI)</h4><p>Successor to RiskIQ PassiveTotal (acquired by Microsoft, 2021). Aggregates passive DNS, WHOIS, SSL, and infrastructure data for attribution and threat hunting.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>PhishTank</h4><p>Community-validated database of phishing URLs; supplies security tools and browsers with up-to-date intelligence.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>Recorded Future</h4><p>Threat-intelligence platform combining machine learning with human analysis across open, dark, and technical sources.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>Microsoft Defender EASM</h4><p>External Attack Surface Management, the renamed RiskIQ Illuminate (acquired by Microsoft, 2021). Discovers and monitors internet-facing assets, third-party services, and shadow IT.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>SANS Institute</h4><p>Training, certification, and research; publishes critical threat research and runs the Internet Storm Center.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>VirusTotal</h4><p>Free analysis of files and URLs across many AV engines; community sharing and reputation data.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>WHOIS</h4><p>Protocol and database of domain registration data; used to investigate suspicious domains and attribution.</p></article>
  </div>
</section>

{/* 2. Cloud */}
<section id="cloud" class="cat-section">
  <header class="cat-section__head">
    <span class="cat-section__ico">☁️</span>
    <div>
      <h2>Cloud &amp; infrastructure monitoring / security</h2>
      <p>Platforms that watch cloud workloads, identities, and posture across multi-account, multi-cloud environments.</p>
    </div>
  </header>
  <div class="cat-tools">
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>AWS CloudTrail</h4><p>Records and logs API activity within an AWS account, the audit trail for security and forensic work.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>AWS CloudWatch</h4><p>Monitoring and observability for AWS resources and applications; metrics, logs, alarms, automation hooks.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>Azure AD (Entra ID)</h4><p>Microsoft's cloud identity and access management; SSO, MFA, conditional access, and integration with SaaS apps.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>Cloudflare Cloudforce One</h4><p>Cloudflare's threat-research team and intelligence service; produces actionable indicators, campaign tracking, and disruption support against active adversaries.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>Forcepoint</h4><p>DLP, cloud security, and insider-threat behavior analytics across endpoints, networks, and cloud applications.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>Google Security Operations (formerly Chronicle)</h4><p>Cloud-native security analytics platform from Google Cloud; high-speed search and threat detection at petabyte scale. Chronicle was folded into the Google Security Operations (Google SecOps) brand in 2024.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>Microsoft Sentinel</h4><p>Cloud-native SIEM/SOAR (formerly Azure Sentinel); AI-driven analytics across on-prem, cloud, and hybrid with rich connector library, plus a built-in UEBA module for user and entity behavior analytics.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>Orca Security</h4><p>Agentless cloud security; full-stack visibility, vulnerability management, and risk prioritization across cloud workloads.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>Prisma Cloud</h4><p>Cloud-native security platform from Palo Alto Networks; workload protection, IaC scanning, compliance, runtime defense.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>Sysdig</h4><p>Cloud-native security focused on container runtime, Kubernetes monitoring, and cloud workload protection.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>WIZ</h4><p>Agentless cloud risk visibility; correlates infra/config/workload data to map attack paths and prioritize risk.</p></article>
  </div>
</section>

{/* 3. SIEM */}
<section id="siem" class="cat-section">
  <header class="cat-section__head">
    <span class="cat-section__ico">📊</span>
    <div>
      <h2>Data collection / logging / SIEM</h2>
      <p>Where the logs go to be aggregated, normalized, and queried. The SOC's primary lens.</p>
    </div>
  </header>
  <div class="cat-tools">
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>ArcSight</h4><p>Long-standing SIEM with strong correlation engine for compliance reporting and forensic analysis.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>Cribl</h4><p>Data routing and processing for observability pipelines; filter, enrich, and reduce log data before forwarding.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>Elastic Security</h4><p>Unified security on the Elastic Stack; endpoint protection, prebuilt detections, and customizable monitoring.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>Exabeam</h4><p>Next-gen SIEM with built-in UEBA; user-behavior timelines and automated investigation workflows.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>Fluentd</h4><p>Open-source data collector that unifies log collection and processing across distributed systems via plugins.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>IBM QRadar</h4><p>Enterprise SIEM with real-time correlation, ML-assisted prioritization, and broad ecosystem integration.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>Logstash</h4><p>Server-side pipeline that ingests, transforms, and forwards data; central to the Elastic Stack.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>SIEM (concept)</h4><p>Aggregation and correlation of security event data for centralized detection, compliance, and IR.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>Splunk Enterprise Security</h4><p>Premium SIEM on the Splunk platform; correlation searches, ML, prebuilt content for SOC workflows.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>Sysmon</h4><p>Windows service that produces rich endpoint telemetry, process creation, network connections, file events, for SIEM ingestion.</p></article>
  </div>
</section>

{/* 4. EDR */}
<section id="edr" class="cat-section">
  <header class="cat-section__head">
    <span class="cat-section__ico">💻</span>
    <div>
      <h2>Endpoint detection &amp; response / endpoint security</h2>
      <p>The agents that watch process trees, file activity, and on-host behavior.</p>
    </div>
  </header>
  <div class="cat-tools">
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>Bitdefender GravityZone</h4><p>EDR with behavior analysis, anti-exploit, root-cause analysis, and policy-based remediation.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>CrowdStrike Falcon</h4><p>Cloud-native EDR with a lightweight agent; combines behavioral analytics, threat intel, and ML.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>Cybereason</h4><p>EDR/XDR focused on endpoint visibility, malware detection, IR, and deep process-tree forensics.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>Darktrace</h4><p>Self-learning AI; builds behavioral baselines, detects anomalies, autonomously responds via its RESPOND module (formerly Antigena).</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>Digital Guardian</h4><p>DLP and endpoint security with context-aware policies for data usage across endpoints, networks, cloud.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>Falcon OverWatch</h4><p>Managed threat-hunting service on top of CrowdStrike Falcon; 24/7 human-led analysis to validate stealthy intrusions.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>Proofpoint Identity Threat Defense (formerly Illusive)</h4><p>Deception-based identity-threat detection. Plants fake credentials and decoy paths to surface attacker lateral movement. Illusive Networks acquired by Proofpoint, 2022.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>Kaspersky EDR</h4><p>Attack-chain visualization, IOC search, sandboxing, and threat-intel integration with centralized management. U.S. sales were prohibited by a June 2024 Commerce Department determination; still widely deployed elsewhere.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>Malwarebytes EDR</h4><p>Lightweight EDR with strong remediation; ransomware rollback and post-infection recovery.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>Microsoft Defender</h4><p>Antivirus, endpoint protection, EDR (Defender for Endpoint), and XDR ties to Sentinel and Entra ID.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>SentinelOne</h4><p>Unified EPP/EDR with behavioral AI, autonomous response, rollback on Windows, and threat-hunting telemetry.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>Sophos Intercept X</h4><p>Next-gen AV plus EDR with deep learning and exploit prevention; strong investigation tools for SMB and enterprise.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>Symantec (Broadcom)</h4><p>Endpoint protection, email security, DLP, and EDR; global sensor network feeding detection and response.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>Trellix Endpoint Security</h4><p>Part of the broader Trellix XDR; detection, investigation, and response with behavioral analytics and forensic timelines.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>Trend Micro Vision One</h4><p>XDR correlating telemetry across email, endpoint, server, and cloud workloads.</p></article>
  </div>
</section>

    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>EnCase Forensic</h4><p>Long-standing digital-investigation suite; acquisition, indexing, analysis, and court-admissible reporting.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>Magnet AXIOM</h4><p>Forensics across computers, mobile, cloud, IoT; powerful artifact parsing and cross-source correlation.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>MSAB</h4><p>Mobile forensics for law enforcement; XRY for extraction, XAMN for evidence visualization, XEC for lab management.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>Oxygen Forensic Detective</h4><p>Mobile, cloud, drone, and IoT forensics with deep parsing of app data including secure messengers.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>X-Ways Forensics</h4><p>Lightweight, fast forensic suite with strong scripting and hex-level analysis for deep investigations.</p></article>
  </div>
</section>

    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>Lansweeper</h4><p>IT asset discovery and inventory; CMDB foundations and continuous visibility into devices, software, and users.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>NetFlow</h4><p>Cisco-developed protocol for flow-based monitoring and anomaly detection; metadata about traffic sessions.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>Nexpose (Rapid7)</h4><p>Vulnerability management with dynamic scans and exploitability-based prioritization; pairs with Metasploit.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>Nessus (Tenable)</h4><p>Vulnerability scanner with authenticated/unauthenticated scans and compliance framework support.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>NIDS (concept)</h4><p>Network intrusion detection: inspects traffic for signatures, protocol anomalies, and behavioral indicators.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>Snort</h4><p>Open-source NIDS with deep packet inspection and a large community rule set maintained by Cisco Talos.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>Suricata</h4><p>Open-source NIDS/IPS with multi-threaded inspection, file extraction, TLS inspection, and JSON output.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>Tripwire</h4><p>File-integrity monitoring and change detection; enforces baselines and supports compliance.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>Wireshark</h4><p>Open-source packet analyzer; deep protocol decoding for investigation and forensics.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>Zeek (formerly Bro)</h4><p>Behavior-focused network monitoring producing structured session logs for SIEM-friendly analytics.</p></article>
  </div>
</section>

{/* 8. SOAR */}
<section id="soar" class="cat-section">
  <header class="cat-section__head">
    <span class="cat-section__ico">🤖</span>
    <div>
      <h2>Incident response / SOAR / automation</h2>
      <p>Where playbooks live, alerts get enriched, and analysts get back their afternoons.</p>
    </div>
  </header>
  <div class="cat-tools">
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>Cynet AutoXDR</h4><p>XDR with autonomous investigation and remediation playbooks aimed at small-to-mid SOCs.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>DFIR ORC</h4><p>Open-source forensic-artifact collector from ANSSI (the French national cyber agency). Runs on Windows endpoints to gather a broad, configurable set of artifacts for offline post-compromise analysis. Not a case-management platform.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>Sumo Logic Cloud SOAR (formerly DFLabs IncMan)</h4><p>SOAR with advanced playbooks, threat-intel enrichment, KPI tracking, and human-in-the-loop decisions. DFLabs acquired by Sumo Logic, 2021.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>Cortex XSOAR (Palo Alto)</h4><p>Comprehensive SOAR with case management, threat intel, playbook-driven automation, and hundreds of integrations.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>ServiceNow SecOps</h4><p>ITSM and security workflow with ticketing, change management, CMDB integration, and security automation.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>Google Security Operations SOAR (formerly Siemplify / Chronicle SOAR)</h4><p>SOAR with case management, playbooks, and analyst dashboards; integrated with Google SecOps (Chronicle) telemetry. Siemplify acquired by Google, 2022; fully integrated into Google SecOps.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>SOAR (concept)</h4><p>Security orchestration, automation, and response: define, automate, and orchestrate IR workflows at scale.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>Splunk SOAR (formerly Splunk Phantom)</h4><p>SOAR with Python-based playbooks, 300+ integrations, and deep Splunk analytics ties. Phantom acquired by Splunk in 2018 and renamed Splunk SOAR in 2021; Splunk itself acquired by Cisco, 2024.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>Swimlane</h4><p>Low-code SOAR for SOC analysts to build automation workflows without deep programming expertise.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>TheHive Project</h4><p>IR platform with collaborative case management; integrates with Cortex for automated analysis. TheHive 5 is a commercial product from StrangeBee; the open-source TheHive 4 reached end of life in December 2022.</p></article>
    <article class="cat-tool"><h4>Tines</h4><p>No-code automation built for security teams; modular "stories" for alert ingestion, enrichment, and response.</p></article>
  </div>
