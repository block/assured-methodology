ASSURED is seven questions asked in order. Alert, Subject and Scope orient the analyst: what fired, who is involved, and where the lines are. Uncover and Risk investigate: what the evidence says, and how bad it is. The Escalation decision and Documentation resolve the case: close or escalate, and write down what was decided and learned.

<SevenQuestions label="The seven ASSURED questions by stage" />

New to the method? Read the [introduction](/docs/intro/) first, then the chapters in order. Each chapter teaches its phase in pillars, works the same cases through it, and ends with a quiz.
