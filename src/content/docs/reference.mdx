## The seven phases

<div class="ref-phases">
  <div class="ref-phase ref-phase--alert">
    <div class="ref-phase__head"><LetterChip letter="A" phase="alert" size="sm" /><a href="/docs/alert/">Alert</a><span class="ref-phase__q">What fired, and why?</span></div>
    <p class="ref-phase__out"><strong>Produce:</strong> the validated alert (detection mechanism and its trust level, normalized fields, validation results) plus flagged leads and temporal anchors.</p>
    <p class="ref-phase__decide"><strong>Decide:</strong> known-benign pattern, discriminators and eligibility gate proven → Level 0 five-field close · no real signal → close as false-positive with detection feedback · validated signal → Subject.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="ref-phase ref-phase--subject">
    <div class="ref-phase__head"><LetterChip letter="S" phase="subject" size="sm" /><a href="/docs/subject/">Subject</a><span class="ref-phase__q">Who is involved?</span></div>
    <p class="ref-phase__out"><strong>Produce:</strong> the entity map (primary, secondary, downstream), per-entity assessment on the four dimensions, baselines with confidence labels, insider indicators (or "none observed").</p>
    <p class="ref-phase__decide"><strong>Decide:</strong> all four dimensions clean and the alert explained → close with evidence · any dimension dirty or unexplained → Scope · insider pattern → engage HR/legal channel, do not tip the subject.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="ref-phase ref-phase--scope">
    <div class="ref-phase__head"><LetterChip letter="S" phase="scope" size="sm" /><a href="/docs/scope/">Scope</a><span class="ref-phase__q">Where are the lines?</span></div>
    <p class="ref-phase__out"><strong>Produce:</strong> the one-page scope: regulatory flags and their clocks, the time window, entity boundaries, and the visibility map (what the tooling cannot see, named).</p>
    <p class="ref-phase__decide"><strong>Decide:</strong> regulated data in reach → start the parallel clocks and engage the owners · boundaries set → Uncover · new evidence later widens the lines → re-scope; the loop is the method working.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="ref-phase ref-phase--uncover">
    <div class="ref-phase__head"><LetterChip letter="U" phase="uncover" size="sm" /><a href="/docs/uncover/">Uncover</a><span class="ref-phase__q">What does the evidence say?</span></div>
    <p class="ref-phase__out"><strong>Produce:</strong> the confidence-labeled timeline, the hypothesis ledger with recorded negatives, and the ATT&CK mapping of the reconstructed chain.</p>
    <p class="ref-phase__decide"><strong>Decide:</strong> evidence supports a benign explanation → Risk with a close recommendation · malicious chain reconstructed → Risk · criterion confirmed mid-phase → escalate now, finish the phases under IR's clock.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="ref-phase ref-phase--risk">
    <div class="ref-phase__head"><LetterChip letter="R" phase="risk" size="sm" /><a href="/docs/risk/">Risk</a><span class="ref-phase__q">How bad is it, and how confident are we?</span></div>
    <p class="ref-phase__out"><strong>Produce:</strong> the impact × likelihood verdict scored on the RATM rubric, the priority from the triage matrix (P1 to P4), and a named confidence level.</p>
    <p class="ref-phase__decide"><strong>Decide:</strong> P1 → escalate immediately, warm handoff · P2 → escalate within the shift · P3 → keep working, re-score within 24 hours on a named trigger (High/Low: the tightened high-impact clock, hours not 24) · P4 → document and close.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="ref-phase ref-phase--escalation">
    <div class="ref-phase__head"><LetterChip letter="E" phase="escalation" size="sm" /><a href="/docs/escalation/">Escalation</a><span class="ref-phase__q">Who needs to know, and with what context?</span></div>
    <p class="ref-phase__out"><strong>Produce:</strong> the close-or-escalate decision against the pre-committed criteria, and (on escalate) the nine-section handoff packet that survives the five-minute cold read.</p>
    <p class="ref-phase__decide"><strong>Decide:</strong> criterion met → escalate through the protocol tier the criterion names · no criterion met → documented close, a first-class verdict · either way, the reasoning goes in the record.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="ref-phase ref-phase--documentation">
    <div class="ref-phase__head"><LetterChip letter="D" phase="documentation" size="sm" /><a href="/docs/documentation/">Documentation</a><span class="ref-phase__q">What did we decide, and what did we learn?</span></div>
    <p class="ref-phase__out"><strong>Produce:</strong> the event report (nine sections), the sanitized share version, links to every related artifact, and the detection-engineering brief.</p>
    <p class="ref-phase__decide"><strong>Decide:</strong> record passes closure review → case closed · reviewer requests clarification → fix the record, not the memory of it · exemplary either way → training archive.</p>
  </div>
</div>

## The escalation checklist

Run at every phase. Any criterion <strong>confirmed</strong> escalates immediately (break-glass, P1); suspicion refines Scope instead. The full checklist with sub-checks is a template: <a href="/templates/escalation-checklist.md" download>⬇ escalation-checklist.md</a>.

<div class="ref-crit">
  <div class="ref-crit__item">✅ <strong>Confirmed malicious activity.</strong> Verified IOCs, malware, exploitation, corroborated ATT&CK-mapped techniques.</div>
  <div class="ref-crit__item">💎 <strong>High impact / business-critical.</strong> Confirmed malicious or unauthorized activity touching production, regulated data, or identity infrastructure; escalate before the evidence picture is complete. Asset profile alone never breaks glass; it tightens the clock (Risk's override rules): missing evidence reads High/Medium (P2, within shift), benign-leaning evidence High/Low (P3, tightened clock).</div>
  <div class="ref-crit__item">↔️ <strong>Lateral movement or exfiltration.</strong> A second host, account, or trust boundary; staging or outbound transfer. Blast radius is growing.</div>
  <div class="ref-crit__item">🌐 <strong>Ongoing or widespread campaign.</strong> Multiple hosts or users, shared infrastructure or lures, coordinated timing.</div>
</div>

## The depth ladder

<div class="ref-ladder">
  <div class="ref-ladder__rung"><strong>Level 0 · Pattern close.</strong> Known-benign pattern, library discriminators pass, eligibility gate proven from source systems (unknown fails it), five-field record. Minutes.</div>
  <div class="ref-ladder__rung"><strong>Level 1 · Standard triage.</strong> The seven phases at working depth. Most validated alerts live here.</div>
  <div class="ref-ladder__rung"><strong>Level 2 · Full arc.</strong> The seven phases at full depth with the nine-section report. Multiple subjects, privilege, regulated data, or anything that survived Level 1 unresolved.</div>
</div>

Any surprise moves the case <strong>up</strong> a level, never down mid-case. Depth and urgency are separate decisions: break-glass escalates even from inside a Level 0 check. Full rules: <a href="/docs/fast-path/">the fast path</a>.
