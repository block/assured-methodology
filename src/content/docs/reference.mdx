<p class="ref-hooks">Memory hooks: the two S's are <strong>who before where</strong>. Risk recommends; <strong>E decides</strong>, and most verdicts close. A confirmed escalation criterion exits from <strong>any</strong> phase (the escalation tripwire). Early exits, a Level 0 close or a tripwire escalation, are early E decisions, recorded in the E fields of the record.</p>

## The seven phases

<div class="ref-phases">
  <div class="ref-phase ref-phase--alert">
    <div class="ref-phase__head"><LetterChip letter="A" phase="alert" size="sm" /><a href="/docs/alert/">Alert</a><span class="ref-phase__q">What fired, and why?</span></div>
    <p class="ref-phase__out"><strong>Produce:</strong> the validated alert (detection mechanism and its trust level, normalized fields, validation results), the depth level with its reason, plus flagged leads, the working hypothesis with its alternatives, known gaps, and temporal anchors.</p>
    <p class="ref-phase__decide"><strong>Decide:</strong> known-benign pattern, discriminators and eligibility gate proven → Level 0 five-field close · rule misread the activity → close as false positive (logic or data) with detection feedback · rule right, activity authorized → benign true positive, close with a pattern-entry proposal or a scoped, expiring exception; the rule stays as written · validated signal → Subject · no rule raised it (a user report, a notification) → ask what was reported, by whom, and what can be verified independently, then run the same moves (<a href="/docs/alert/intake/">other intake</a>).</p>
  </div>
  <div class="ref-phase ref-phase--subject">
    <div class="ref-phase__head"><LetterChip letter="S" phase="subject" size="sm" /><a href="/docs/subject/">Subject</a><span class="ref-phase__q">Who is involved?</span></div>
    <p class="ref-phase__out"><strong>Produce:</strong> the entity map (primary, secondary, downstream), the relationship graph (trust paths, lateral-movement candidates), per-entity assessment on the four dimensions, baselines with confidence labels, insider indicators from observable technical activity (or "none observed").</p>
    <p class="ref-phase__decide"><strong>Decide:</strong> all four dimensions clean and the alert explained → record the clean verdict explicitly and hand Scope a narrow recommendation; the remaining phases collapse into single moves (Level 1) toward a documented close at E · any dimension dirty or unexplained → Scope · insider pattern → route it through the insider-risk process, do not tip the subject.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="ref-phase ref-phase--scope">
    <div class="ref-phase__head"><LetterChip letter="S" phase="scope" size="sm" /><a href="/docs/scope/">Scope</a><span class="ref-phase__q">Where are the lines?</span></div>
    <p class="ref-phase__out"><strong>Produce:</strong> the one-page scope: regulatory flags and their clocks, the time window, entity boundaries, and the visibility map (what the tooling cannot see, named).</p>
    <p class="ref-phase__decide"><strong>Decide:</strong> regulated data in reach → start the parallel clocks and engage the owners · boundaries set → Uncover · new evidence later widens the lines → re-scope; the loop is the method working.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="ref-phase ref-phase--uncover">
    <div class="ref-phase__head"><LetterChip letter="U" phase="uncover" size="sm" /><a href="/docs/uncover/">Uncover</a><span class="ref-phase__q">What does the evidence say?</span></div>
    <p class="ref-phase__out"><strong>Produce:</strong> the confidence-labeled timeline, the hypothesis ledger with recorded negatives, and the ATT&CK mapping of the reconstructed chain.</p>
    <p class="ref-phase__decide"><strong>Decide:</strong> evidence supports a benign explanation → Risk with a close recommendation · malicious chain reconstructed → Risk · criterion confirmed mid-phase → escalate now (escalation tripwire), then work only the scoping tasks the IR owner assigns.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="ref-phase ref-phase--risk">
    <div class="ref-phase__head"><LetterChip letter="R" phase="risk" size="sm" /><a href="/docs/risk/">Risk</a><span class="ref-phase__q">How bad is it, and how confident are we?</span></div>
    <p class="ref-phase__out"><strong>Produce:</strong> the impact rating, the likelihood rating (how probable it is that the activity is malicious or unauthorized), and the analytic confidence in that judgment, scored on the RATM rubric (ASSURED's Risk-Based Alert Triage Matrix); the matrix cell; the priority (P1 to P4); and a recommended move.</p>
    <p class="ref-phase__decide"><strong>Recommend:</strong> P1 → escalate immediately, warm handoff · P2 → escalate within 4 hours · P3 → keep working to the named re-score trigger, re-score within 24 hours (High/Low: within hours; Low/High: confirm and close) · P4 → documented close. E makes and records the decision.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="ref-phase ref-phase--escalation">
    <div class="ref-phase__head"><LetterChip letter="E" phase="escalation" size="sm" /><a href="/docs/escalation/">Escalation decision</a><span class="ref-phase__q">Close or escalate, and who needs to know?</span></div>
    <p class="ref-phase__out"><strong>Produce:</strong> the close-or-escalate decision, recorded with the matrix cell, and (on escalate) the nine-section handoff packet (summary, timeline, entities, evidence chain, risk verdict, containment, artifacts, open questions, communication record) that a responder with no context can act on within five minutes.</p>
    <p class="ref-phase__decide"><strong>Decide:</strong> criterion confirmed, or P1/P2 → escalate through the tier the protocol names · P3 → keep working to the named re-score trigger, then decide · P4 → documented close, a first-class verdict · either way, the reasoning goes in the record.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="ref-phase ref-phase--documentation">
    <div class="ref-phase__head"><LetterChip letter="D" phase="documentation" size="sm" /><a href="/docs/documentation/">Documentation</a><span class="ref-phase__q">What did we decide, and what did we learn?</span></div>
    <p class="ref-phase__out"><strong>Produce:</strong> the record for the rung (the five-field record at Level 0, the alert report at Level 1, the nine-section event report at Level 2), links to every related artifact, and the detection-engineering feedback (a <a href="/docs/detection-feedback/#the-tuning-request">tuning request</a> or a pattern-entry proposal).</p>
    <p class="ref-phase__decide"><strong>Decide:</strong> record passes review (per-case closure review at Level 2; Level 0 and 1 closes through the weekly QA sample) → case closed · reviewer requests clarification → fix the record, not the memory of it · exemplary either way → training archive.</p>
  </div>
</div>

## The escalation checklist

Run at every phase. Any criterion <strong>confirmed</strong> (supported at High confidence on the <a href="#the-confidence-rubric">confidence rubric</a>) escalates immediately under the escalation tripwire and makes the case P1; suspicion refines Scope instead. The full checklist with sub-checks is a template: <a href="/templates/escalation-checklist.md" download><span aria-hidden="true">⬇ </span>escalation-checklist.md</a>.

<div class="ref-crit">
  <div class="ref-crit__item"><strong>1 · Confirmed malicious activity not fully contained by a control.</strong> Verified IOCs (indicators of compromise), malware, exploitation, corroborated ATT&CK-mapped techniques. Blocked or quarantined by a control with no execution or follow-on evidence is contained by control: P3 or P4 and a pattern-entry candidate, not a page.</div>
  <div class="ref-crit__item"><strong>2 · High impact / business-critical.</strong> Confirmed malicious or unauthorized activity on production, regulated data, or identity infrastructure: escalate before the picture is complete. Asset profile alone never trips the escalation tripwire; it tightens the Risk clock (missing evidence → High/Medium, P2 within 4 hours; benign-leaning → High/Low, P3, hours not 24).</div>
  <div class="ref-crit__item"><strong>3 · Lateral movement or exfiltration.</strong> Attacker activity reaching a second host, account, or trust boundary; staging or outbound transfer. Blast radius is growing. Signs: credential dumping (T1003) followed by pass-the-hash or pass-the-ticket; internal discovery (net, nltest, AD cmdlets, BloodHound); remote execution with PsExec or WMI from a non-admin workstation; tunneling; large or unusual outbound transfers or staged archives.</div>
  <div class="ref-crit__item"><strong>4 · Ongoing or widespread campaign.</strong> Multiple hosts or users, shared infrastructure or lures, coordinated timing.</div>
</div>

<p class="ref-note">Operational route, no criterion required: a single analyst who cannot both watch the queue and work a confirmed case escalates on capacity. Name the route in the handoff.</p>

<p class="ref-sign"><strong>Before you sign:</strong> can you point to the raw field behind each claim? Can you name the result that would change your mind? Did any of this come from an AI draft, and did you verify it against raw telemetry?</p>

## The confidence rubric

<div class="ref-conf">
  <div class="ref-conf__item"><strong>HIGH:</strong> direct evidence from a source trusted for exactly this kind of claim, corroborated by at least one independent source, with the plausible alternatives tested and ruled out. A criterion is confirmed only here.</div>
  <div class="ref-conf__item"><strong>MEDIUM:</strong> single-source evidence or strong inference. Alternatives are plausible but less likely, and nothing found so far contradicts the claim.</div>
  <div class="ref-conf__item"><strong>LOW:</strong> circumstantial, incomplete, or resting on the absence of contrary evidence. Gaps material to the claim remain open.</div>
</div>

<p class="ref-note"><strong>Usage:</strong> label the claim, not the case · between two levels, round the label down (never the likelihood) · record what kept a malicious claim from HIGH as an open question and close it first · unknown is not low: evidence that could not be examined means low confidence, never low likelihood. Full rubric: <a href="/docs/documentation/standards/#confidence">Documentation standards</a>.</p>

## The regulatory clocks

The analyst recognizes, timestamps and routes; counsel and the privacy owners decide notification. Commit the recognition timestamp to the record the moment regulated data is plausibly in reach.

## The depth ladder

<div class="ref-ladder">
  <div class="ref-ladder__rung"><strong>Level 0 · Pattern close, ≤ 5 minutes.</strong> Known-benign pattern on a governed library entry, discriminators pass, eligibility gate proven from source systems (unknown fails it; privileged identities always fail it), five-field record.</div>
  <div class="ref-ladder__rung"><strong>Level 1 · Standard triage, about 15 to 45 minutes.</strong> The seven phases at working depth. The default rung for cases with no matching pattern and a bounded blast radius.</div>
  <div class="ref-ladder__rung"><strong>Level 2 · Full arc, about 25 to 80 minutes for a senior analyst.</strong> The seven phases at full depth with the nine-section event report. Crown-jewel subject, privileged identity, regulated data plausibly accessed by the activity, a second subject, a novel detection or correlated alert chain, or anything that survived Level 1 unresolved.</div>
</div>

Any surprise moves the case <strong>up</strong> a level, never down mid-case. The case is the unit: grouped alerts take the level of their riskiest member. Depth and urgency are separate decisions: the escalation tripwire fires even from inside a Level 0 check. Full rules: <a href="/docs/fast-path/">the fast path</a>.

## The closure review

Level 2, P1/P2 and escalated-then-closed records each get this review; Level 0 and 1 closes are covered by the weekly QA sample. The reviewer accepts the record, requests clarification, or flags it as a training case.

<ul class="ref-review">
  <li>Decision header complete: both verdict axes in the fixed vocabulary, the Escalation decision with who and when, the priority with its cell or override.</li>
  <li>Every factual claim cites its source: a query and time range, a log record ID, or an artifact hash.</li>
  <li>Confidence labeled per claim on the rubric above, not once for the case.</li>
  <li>Negative findings name what was examined, not just "nothing found."</li>
  <li>Timeline in UTC with the date; each entry has an actor, a rationale and an outcome.</li>
  <li>Open questions from the handoff resolved or carried forward.</li>
  <li>Material AI use declared, "none" included.</li>
  <li>Benign and authorized closes state their reopen conditions.</li>
</ul>
