# R: Risk - Identifying Risk

The Risk phase is the fifth component of the ASSURED methodology, focusing on quantitatively assessing potential impact and threat severity to enable priority-based response and decision-making. This phase transforms the evidence gathered in the Uncover phase into actionable risk assessments that guide response priorities and resource allocation.

## Overview

The Risk phase provides structured risk assessment through:
- Quantitative impact and likelihood evaluation
- Threat severity assessment and prioritization
- Business impact quantification
- Risk-based response planning
- Decision support for escalation and resource allocation

## Framework for Risk Assessment

A structured risk assessment framework ensures consistent, objective evaluation of security events across different scenarios and organizational contexts.

### Risk Assessment Components

**Impact Assessment**
- Business operations disruption
- Financial loss estimation
- Regulatory and compliance impact
- Reputational damage assessment
- Data exposure and privacy impact

**Likelihood Evaluation**
- Threat actor capability assessment
- Vulnerability exploitation probability
- Attack success likelihood
- Detection and response effectiveness
- Mitigation control effectiveness

**Risk Calculation**
- Risk = Impact × Likelihood
- Qualitative and quantitative approaches
- Risk scoring and categorization
- Priority ranking and comparison
- Risk tolerance and acceptance criteria

### Risk Categories

**Critical Risk (Score: 9-10)**
- Immediate threat to business operations
- Significant financial impact (>$1M)
- Regulatory notification required
- High probability of success
- Limited mitigation options

**High Risk (Score: 7-8)**
- Major operational impact
- Substantial financial loss ($100K-$1M)
- Compliance implications
- Moderate to high success probability
- Some mitigation controls available

**Medium Risk (Score: 4-6)**
- Limited operational impact
- Moderate financial loss ($10K-$100K)
- Minor compliance issues
- Moderate success probability
- Effective mitigation controls available

**Low Risk (Score: 1-3)**
- Minimal operational impact
- Low financial loss (&lt;$10K)
- No compliance implications
- Low success probability
- Strong mitigation controls in place

## Working Example: Risk Assessment

### Scenario: PowerShell Execution Investigation

**Investigation Findings:**
- Confirmed malicious PowerShell execution
- Data exfiltration attempt identified
- Lateral movement indicators found
- Attribution to known threat actor group

**Impact Assessment:**

**Business Operations:**
- Limited immediate impact (isolated workstation)
- Potential for broader network compromise
- Customer data at risk
- Operational disruption: Low-Medium

**Financial Impact:**
- Direct costs: Investigation and remediation ($50K)
- Potential data breach costs: $500K-$2M
- Regulatory notification costs: $25K
- Total estimated impact: $575K-$2.075M

**Regulatory Impact:**
- No immediate notification requirements
- Potential GDPR implications if PII accessed
- Industry compliance considerations
- Audit and investigation requirements

**Data and Privacy:**
- System administrator account compromised
- Access to sensitive systems possible
- Customer data exposure risk
- Intellectual property at risk

**Likelihood Assessment:**

**Threat Actor Capability:**
- Known advanced persistent threat group
- High technical sophistication
- Significant resources and funding
- Historical success in similar attacks
- Capability: High

**Vulnerability Exploitation:**
- Successful initial access achieved
- Privilege escalation demonstrated
- Lateral movement attempted
- Data exfiltration initiated
- Exploitation: High

**Attack Success Factors:**
- Initial access: Achieved
- Privilege escalation: Successful
- Lateral movement: In progress
- Data exfiltration: Attempted
- Persistence: Likely established

**Defense Effectiveness:**
- Detection: Effective (alert generated)
- Response: Rapid (within hours)
- Containment: Successful
- Recovery: In progress
- Effectiveness: Good

**Risk Calculation:**
- Impact Score: 8 (High financial and operational impact)
- Likelihood Score: 8 (High probability of success)
- Risk Score: 8 × 8 = 64 (High Risk)
- Priority: Immediate response required

## Risk-Based Decision Making

### Response Prioritization

**Critical Risk Events**
- Immediate containment and isolation
- Executive notification and involvement
- External support and resources
- 24/7 monitoring and response
- Regulatory notification preparation

**High Risk Events**
- Rapid containment and assessment
- Management notification
- Dedicated response team
- Enhanced monitoring
- Regular status updates

**Medium Risk Events**
- Standard response procedures
- Team lead notification
- Normal business hours response
- Routine monitoring
- Standard documentation

**Low Risk Events**
- Basic response procedures
- Local team handling
- Minimal escalation
- Standard monitoring
- Routine documentation

### Resource Allocation

**Resource Prioritization**
- High-risk events receive priority resources
- Critical skills and expertise allocation
- Tool and technology access
- External vendor support
- Management attention and oversight

**Budget and Cost Considerations**
- Risk-based budget allocation
- Cost-benefit analysis
- ROI of security investments
- Insurance and coverage optimization
- Long-term risk management planning

## Transition to Escalation Phase

After completing the Risk assessment, the investigation transitions to the **Escalation** phase, where analysts will:

- Determine appropriate escalation paths based on risk assessment
- Implement risk-based response priorities
- Coordinate with stakeholders based on risk levels
- Plan for ongoing risk management and monitoring

The comprehensive risk assessment provides the foundation for informed decision-making and appropriate escalation in the Escalation phase.

## Key Takeaways

1. **Structured Assessment**: Consistent framework ensures objective risk evaluation
2. **Quantitative Analysis**: Financial and probability assessment enables data-driven decisions
3. **Business Context**: Risk assessment must consider business impact and priorities
4. **Stakeholder Communication**: Clear risk communication enables informed decision-making
5. **Continuous Monitoring**: Risk assessment is an ongoing process, not a one-time event

The Risk phase provides the critical bridge between investigation findings and response actions, ensuring that security events are addressed with appropriate priority and resource allocation based on comprehensive risk assessment.
