<ChapterExample title="Turning evidence into a defensible priority" scenario="Uncover delivered: phishing-driven intrusion, two confirmed hosts compromised, federated SSO into a cloud finance platform from a stolen identity. PCI and GDPR triggers were active during Scope. Risk now sets the priority.">

<ExampleStepper steps={["Score impact", "Score likelihood", "Combine via matrix", "Document the verdict", "Hand off to Escalation"]}>

  <div data-step class="example-step">
    <h3>Step 1. Score impact</h3>
    <p><strong>High.</strong> Two hosts compromised. A federated SSO session reached a payment-processing platform. PCI and GDPR triggers are already active. If the adversary moves further, financial and regulatory consequences are direct. Even at the current evidence level, impact is high.</p>
  </div>

  <div data-step class="example-step">
    <h3>Step 2. Score likelihood</h3>
    <p><strong>High.</strong> Multiple independent sources confirm: EDR process tree, network connection to a domain registered 4 days ago, federated identity SSO, mailbox audit on three users. The chain maps cleanly to a known phishing-with-macros pattern. No legitimate explanation has survived investigation. Likelihood is high.</p>
  </div>

  <div data-step class="example-step">
    <h3>Step 3. Combine via the matrix</h3>
    <p>High impact × high likelihood = <strong>Escalate immediately.</strong> The matrix says P1. The decision is automatic; the work is to produce the handoff package for incident response.</p>
  </div>

  <div data-step class="example-step">
    <h3>Step 4. Document the verdict</h3>
    <p>The Risk verdict records:</p>
    <ul class="example-step__list">
      <li>Impact: <strong>High.</strong> Two hosts compromised; potential reach into PCI-scoped payment platform.</li>
      <li>Likelihood: <strong>High.</strong> Multi-source evidence; clean ATT&CK mapping.</li>
      <li>Priority: <strong>P1.</strong> Escalate immediately.</li>
      <li>Open questions: vendor-side activity on the cloud platform pending; mobile device activity may be unmonitored.</li>
      <li>Recommended response: contain the two confirmed hosts, rotate dlin's credentials, isolate the federated SSO session, notify compliance.</li>
    </ul>
  </div>

  <div data-step class="example-step">
    <h3>Step 5. Hand off to Escalation</h3>
    <p>Risk's deliverable is a one-page verdict the Escalation phase can act on. Impact and likelihood justified separately, the matrix decision shown, open questions named, recommended response sketched. The next analyst (Tier 2 / incident responder) has everything they need to start.</p>
  </div>

</ExampleStepper>

</ChapterExample>

<KeyTakeaway>Risk turned three days of detection and three hours of investigation into a one-page verdict. The verdict is what travels to Escalation. The reasoning behind it is what defends the case six months later.</KeyTakeaway>
