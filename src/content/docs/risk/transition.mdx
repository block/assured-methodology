## The handoff contract

<dl class="handoff-contract">
  <dt>Deliverable</dt>
  <dd>The <a href="/docs/risk/impact-likelihood/#verdict-block">Risk verdict block</a>: impact, likelihood and confidence with their rationale, the cell and priority, any override that fired, open questions and a recommended move. If the case escalates, it becomes section 5 of the <a href="/templates/handoff-packet.md" download>handoff packet</a>.</dd>
  <dt>Close path</dt>
  <dd>P4 recommends a documented close. P3 keeps working to its named re-score trigger, then recommends close or escalate. An <a href="/docs/risk/framework/#undetermined">undetermined</a> verdict names its gaps and a re-score trigger; it never closes silently.</dd>
  <dt>What the next phase asks</dt>
  <dd>Close or escalate, and who needs to know? The Escalation decision applies one rule: escalate if a criterion is confirmed or the matrix places the case at P1 or P2; at P3, keep working to the trigger, then decide; close at P4. The <a href="/docs/escalation/criteria/">four canonical criteria</a> and the <a href="/docs/escalation/handoff/">handoff packet</a> live there.</dd>
  <dt>Depth</dt>
  <dd>A Level 0 close never reaches this handoff: it is an early Escalation decision recorded in the five-field record. Level 1 hands over a few lines; Level 2 hands over the full RATM record.</dd>
  <dt>You are ready if</dt>
  <dd>
    <ul>
      <li>a reviewer who has not seen the case could read your cell, band and recommended move and reach the same call;</li>
      <li>nothing that went unexamined is scored Low, and every P3 or undetermined case has a re-score trigger with a time.</li>
    </ul>
  </dd>
</dl>

## The loop ends here, or it doesn't

For an *unresolved* case, the [Scope → Uncover → Risk loop](/docs/risk/) ends when the residual risk is understood well enough to justify a resolution. If the evidence is still ambiguous, refine the scope and run it again, but on the clock the matrix set: a High-impact case whose uncertainty comes from missing evidence is already P2 and escalates within 4 hours while the loop continues.

The loop is never a reason to sit on a confirmed criterion. A loop that is *confirming* new <DefineTerm term="Lateral Movement" display="lateral movement" /> or <DefineTerm term="Data Staging" display="data staging" /> has already answered the escalation question: the [escalation tripwire](/docs/escalation/criteria/#tripwire) fires, IR takes ownership, and the loop keeps running as scoping support, as tasks the IR owner assigns, under IR's clock. Suspicion iterates; confirmation escalates from wherever the investigation happens to be standing.

<KeyTakeaway>
Escalation follows confirmation and the priority band, not investigative completeness. Risk hands over a scored case and a recommendation; the Escalation decision makes the call, closes with a documented record or escalates, and the remaining work continues under IR's clock.
</KeyTakeaway>
