## How to use this page

[Regulatory boundaries](/docs/scope/regulatory/) teaches the four moves: recognize, commit the timestamps, preserve, get the owner in. This page is the lookup table behind the first two. Use it in three steps.

1. **Recognize.** Match what you can see in the case (the "What you see" column) against the rows. A plausible match is enough. You are flagging a candidate, not deciding applicability.
2. **Flag.** Record the regime, the data class or trigger, the candidate start timestamp in UTC, and the owner you engaged and when, in the [regulatory flags block](#flags-block). Your timestamp is a candidate; the owner decides which moment counts.
3. **Route.** Engage the owner in the "Decision owner" column. For any clock measured in hours, that means the same hour, through the paging path in the [escalation protocols](/docs/escalation/protocols/), not a ticket for the morning.

Four things stay outside the analyst's job: deciding that a regime applies, deciding that notification is required, contacting a regulator or law enforcement, and calling anything a "breach" in the record. The last one is a legal conclusion; write what you observed (see [writing for discovery](/docs/documentation/standards/#discovery)).

### What "the clock starts at" means

Regimes start their clocks at different moments, and few of them wait for confirmation.

- **Awareness or discovery.** <DefineTerm term="General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)" display="GDPR" />, <DefineTerm term="UK GDPR" />, NIS2, the <DefineTerm term="Cyber Resilience Act (CRA)" display="CRA" />, HIPAA, <DefineTerm term="Regulation S-P (SEC)" display="Regulation S-P" /> and most state laws. Often reached during triage. Under GDPR, <DefineTerm term="Awareness (GDPR)" display="awareness" /> is a reasonable degree of certainty that a security incident has compromised personal data. Under HIPAA, <DefineTerm term="Discovery (HIPAA)" display="discovery" /> is the first day anyone in the workforce other than the wrongdoer knew, or would have known with reasonable diligence.
- **Suspicion.** Card-brand rules run from a reasonable suspicion of account-data compromise.
- **A determination or classification.** The US banking rule, <DefineTerm term="NYDFS Part 500 (23 NYCRR 500)" display="NYDFS 500.17" />, Texas and Colorado run from the organization's determination that an incident occurred; DORA's four hours run from classifying the incident as major. Those decisions must be made promptly, and your timestamps are the evidence that they were.
- **Materiality.** <DefineTerm term="SEC Cybersecurity Disclosure Rule" display="SEC Form 8-K Item 1.05" /> runs from the <DefineTerm term="Materiality (SEC)" display="materiality determination" />, which itself must come without unreasonable delay after discovery.
- **Receipt of someone else's notice.** A processor's notice to its controller, a vendor's notice to you, or a law-enforcement call can be the start (see [external notifications](/docs/alert/intake/#external-notifications)).
- **A payment.** Ransom and <DefineTerm term="Extortion Payment Notice" display="extortion payment reports" /> run from the payment.

## Parallel clocks

One event can start several clocks, each with its own start, deadline and owner. They run at the same time, not in sequence, and none of them waits for the investigation to finish. The SOC does not track the clocks; it keeps the timestamp record every clock owner will draw on.

[Case A, the finance-team intrusion](/docs/scope/example/#case-a), shows the pattern. At 09:42 UTC the analyst recognizes that the compromised user can reach a cloud finance reporting platform holding personal data of vendor contacts in the EU and possibly card data. At 09:45, compliance and the DPO are in the case.

| Candidate clock | What the analyst recorded | Who decides and tracks it |
|---|---|---|
| GDPR Art. 33 (72 hours from awareness) | 09:42: personal data of vendor contacts in the EU in reach; no platform activity seen yet | DPO. Reach alone is not awareness; a later finding of attacker activity on the platform may be |
| Card-brand rules (from suspicion) | 09:42: card data in reach *if* the platform touches it; compliance owns that question | Acquirer-relationship owner |
| SEC 8-K Item 1.05 (if the company is listed) | Business functions affected and when each fact was established | Disclosure committee, through counsel |
| Customer and vendor contracts | Which customers' or vendors' data the platform holds | Legal and the account owners |

Three rules keep parallel clocks from colliding.

- **The shortest clock sets the pace.** If any candidate clock is measured in hours (CERT-In, DORA, NIS2, NYDFS's ransom notice, a 24-hour contract), every owner is engaged in that window, not only the one whose clock it is.
- **One timestamp record, many readers.** Record each candidate start once, in UTC, with what you knew at that moment. Each owner reads the same record and decides which moment counts for their regime.
- **Each clock has one named owner.** The [handoff packet](/docs/escalation/handoff/) carries the flags and the owners engaged, so incident response inherits the clocks with the case and nobody assumes someone else filed.

## Special cases

### Law-enforcement delay requests

Several regimes let law enforcement delay notice to individuals: California, New York and Washington state law, HIPAA (§164.412), and the US banking agencies' guidance (a written request). SEC Item 1.05 has its own route through the US Attorney General. Do not assume a delay exists elsewhere, and never act on one yourself: a delay request goes to counsel, who confirms it, records it and tells each clock owner. If a caller asks you to hold off on visible containment, that is a decision for counsel and the IR lead (see [special routes](/docs/escalation/communication/#special-routes)).

### Ransom and extortion payments

A payment starts its own clocks: NYDFS 500.17 (24 hours, then a 30-day written explanation), Australia's Part 3 report (72 hours), and CIRCIA once its rule takes effect (24 hours). Payment decisions belong to legal, executives and the insurer, with sanctions screening against Treasury's OFAC lists (see [external parties](/docs/escalation/protocols/#external-parties)). The analyst's part is to record when the demand arrived, when IR and legal were told, and to [preserve](/docs/escalation/preserve/) the demand and its channel as evidence. The SOC never talks to the threat actor.

### Child sexual abuse material

Stop. Do not view it further, copy, download or forward it, even to a colleague or into the case. Record only the metadata the tooling already shows (location, hash, time), and route it to counsel and the organization's legal reporting channel. In the US, providers of electronic communication or remote computing services must report apparent child sexual abuse material to NCMEC's CyberTipline ([18 U.S.C. §2258A](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/18/2258A)), and the report is treated as a request to preserve for one year. Counsel decides whether that duty applies to you. The analyst is rotated off the case and offered support (see [cognitive traps](/docs/cognitive-traps/)).

<h2 id="flags-block">The regulatory flags block</h2>

American Express, [Data Security Operating Policy](https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/us/merchant/new-data-security/DSOP_United_States_EN.pdf), Section 3. Mastercard's current Security Rules and Procedures could not be retrieved, so its row says "verify".
- **Elsewhere:** [CERT-In Directions No. 20(3)/2022](https://www.cert-in.org.in/PDF/CERT-In_Directions_70B_28.04.2022.pdf). Australia, Department of Home Affairs [ransomware payment reporting factsheet](https://www.homeaffairs.gov.au/cyber-security-subsite/files/factsheet-ransomware-payment-reporting.pdf).

<KeyTakeaway>
Recognize, flag, route. Match what the case shows against the table, record each candidate start in UTC with what you knew at the time, and get every owner in within the window of the shortest clock. Clocks start at awareness, discovery, suspicion, a determination, or a payment, and several of them run in hours. Notification is decided by privacy, legal and compliance; the speed at which they hear about it is decided in triage.
</KeyTakeaway>
