    prompt="A hospital SOC confirms a compromised workstation had access to systems holding PHI. When does HIPAA's 60-day individual-notification clock start?"
    options={[

    options={[
      { value: "inregion", label: "Check transfer and residency limits before moving data; the work may run in-region or via a regional team." },

    answer="inregion"

    prompt="Scope wants six months of SaaS audit history for a user, but the platform keeps 90 days. The query for the older period returns no rows. What goes in the record?"
    options={[

    prompt="Your company hosts a customer's personal data as their processor. Scope finds that an attacker plausibly read some of it. What does GDPR Article 33 expect from your side?"
    options={[
      { value: "authority", label: "Notify the supervisory authority within 72 hours yourselves, because the breach happened on your systems." },
      { value: "nothing", label: "Nothing until the customer asks; as a processor, breach notification is wholly the controller's business." },
      { value: "controller", label: "Tell the controller without undue delay; their 72 hours generally start from your notice to them." },
      { value: "confirm", label: "Wait until exfiltration is confirmed, then send the customer one complete and accurate breach report." },
    ]}
    answer="controller"
    explanation="Article 33(2) gives a processor one duty: notify the controller without undue delay after becoming aware of the breach. The controller is generally treated as aware once its processor tells it, so its 72 hours start from your notice, and every hour your side sits on the finding comes out of the customer's window. Many data-processing agreements set a shorter contractual deadline that runs from your discovery. Notifying the authority is the controller's call (Art. 33(1)), not yours. Waiting for exfiltration repeats the confirmation error: unauthorized access is already a breach of confidentiality. Scope's part is the same as always: commit the timestamp, and get the privacy owner who holds the agreement into the case."
