ASSURED is one method read by very different people. An analyst with an alert open needs the desk card now; a new analyst needs the chapters in order; a SOC lead needs the operating pages. Pick the route that matches why you are here. Every route ends in the same seven questions.

## On shift now

You have an alert open and minutes, not hours.

1. [The one-page reference](/docs/reference/): the seven questions, the escalation criteria, the verdict vocabulary and the priority bands on one card.
2. [The fast path](/docs/fast-path/): pick the depth (Level 0, 1 or 2) and run the eligibility gate before you close anything.
3. When an identity is involved, the [identity-persistence sweep](/docs/reference/identity-sweep/).

</section>

<section class="path-card" aria-labelledby="learning-triage">

## Learning triage

Read the [introduction](/docs/intro/), then the seven chapters in order. Each chapter closes with a worked example on the same cases and a quiz. Reading times cover each chapter's pages except the quiz, at a steady 230 words a minute; a first read with the exercises takes longer.
