After completing the Subject analysis, the investigation transitions to the **Scope** phase, where analysts will:

- Define investigation boundaries and parameters
- Determine regulatory requirements and compliance needs
- Establish time-based and entity-based scopes
- Plan tool integration and data collection strategies

The comprehensive understanding of entities gained in the Subject phase provides the foundation for effective scope definition and subsequent investigation phases.

---

## How Subject Analysis Informs Scoping

The entity profiles, behavioral baselines, and relationship mappings developed during Subject analysis directly inform scoping decisions:

### 🎯 **Entity-Based Scope Definition**
- **High-Risk Entities**: Prioritize accounts, endpoints, and services identified as compromised or suspicious
- **Relationship Networks**: Include connected entities that may have been affected by lateral movement
- **Privilege Boundaries**: Expand scope to cover all systems accessible to compromised privileged accounts
- **Data Access Patterns**: Include data repositories and applications accessed by suspicious entities

### ⏰ **Time-Based Scope Planning**
- **Activity Timeline**: Use authentication logs and behavior analysis to establish investigation time windows
- **Baseline Deviations**: Focus on periods when entities showed significant behavioral changes
- **Historical Context**: Include sufficient historical data to establish normal vs. anomalous patterns
- **Incident Progression**: Trace the evolution of suspicious activity to understand attack progression

### 🔍 **Investigation Depth Requirements**
- **Critical Entity Focus**: Deeper analysis for administrative accounts, domain controllers, and sensitive endpoints
- **Compliance Considerations**: Expanded scope for entities handling regulated data (PHI, PCI, SOX)
- **Business Impact Assessment**: Prioritize investigation of entities supporting critical business functions
- **Attack Surface Mapping**: Include all potential attack vectors identified through relationship analysis

## Key Scope Transition Deliverables

Before transitioning to the Scope phase, ensure the following Subject analysis outputs are complete:

### ✅ **Entity Inventory and Classification**
- Complete catalog of all entities involved in the security event
- Risk classification based on privileges, access, and behavioral anomalies
- Relationship mapping showing connections between suspicious entities
- Baseline behavioral profiles for normal vs. anomalous activity comparison

### ✅ **Authentication and Authorization Assessment**
- Authentication anomaly summary with geographic and temporal analysis
- Authorization violations and privilege misuse documentation
- Multi-factor authentication bypass attempts or failures
- Service account and shared account activity evaluation

### ✅ **Behavioral Analysis Results**
- Historical behavior baseline establishment (typically 90+ days)
- Deviation analysis highlighting significant behavioral changes
- Peer group comparison results for contextual validation
- Privilege escalation and scope creep identification

### ✅ **Relationship and Impact Analysis**
- Network of affected or potentially affected entities
- Lateral movement path identification and risk assessment
- Critical asset exposure through entity relationships
- Business process impact assessment based on entity roles

## Recommended Next Steps

### 1. **Scope Phase Preparation**
Review the Subject analysis findings with key stakeholders to ensure alignment on:
- Investigation priorities based on entity risk classification
- Regulatory and compliance requirements triggered by affected entities
- Business continuity considerations for critical entity types
- Resource allocation for deep-dive analysis of high-priority entities

### 2. **Tool and Data Collection Planning**
Use entity analysis results to plan:
- Log collection strategies focused on identified entities
- Forensic imaging priorities for suspected compromised endpoints
- Network monitoring scope based on relationship analysis
- Identity and access management system queries for privilege verification

### 3. **Stakeholder Communication**
Prepare preliminary findings summary including:
- High-level entity compromise assessment
- Initial impact scope based on entity analysis
- Recommended investigation priorities and timelines
- Resource requirements for the upcoming Scope phase
