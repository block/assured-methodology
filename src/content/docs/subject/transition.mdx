# What Subject hands forward

<div class="deliverable-list">
  <div class="deliverable-item">
    <h4>🗺️ The entity map</h4>
    <p>Every identity touched by the activity: primary, secondary, and high-value paths. Per-entity assessment across the four dimensions. The map is the artifact Scope works from.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="deliverable-item">
    <h4>📊 The baselines used</h4>
    <p>Which baseline informed each behavioral judgment, and what the actual values were at the time of triage. That data defends the verdict in any future review.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="deliverable-item">
    <h4>🔗 The relationship graph</h4>
    <p>Connections between entities that may matter for lateral-movement analysis: trust paths, authentication chains, downstream system access.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="deliverable-item">
    <h4>🟡 Insider indicators</h4>
    <p>Whether any insider risk or threat patterns appeared during Subject. Even a "none observed" note is worth recording because it documents the check happened.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<Callout variant="info" title="The Scope phase will need">
- An asset criticality map for every primary entity (to weight investigation effort).
- A baseline of what is "in scope" for the organization's tooling (data sources available, retention windows, geographic limits).
- Any regulatory or contractual constraints the investigation has to respect.
- A target end-state: what evidence Scope needs Uncover to gather.
</Callout>

<KeyTakeaway>
Subject produced the map. Scope draws the lines. The cleaner the map, the cleaner the lines.
</KeyTakeaway>
