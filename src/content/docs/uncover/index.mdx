<Callout variant="info" title="What Uncover is for">
Alert, Subject, and Scope told the analyst what fired, who was involved, and where the lines are. Uncover is where the analyst actually answers the question: what is going on, what did the adversary do, and what did they touch? Uncover ends with a narrative the analyst can defend.
</Callout>

# Why Uncover deserves its own phase

<div class="why-subject-grid">
  <FlipCard icon="📥" title="Evidence is plural" accent="orange">
    Endpoint telemetry alone is rarely enough. Network logs alone are rarely enough. Uncover is the phase that brings sources together so the analyst can answer a question the way only correlated data can.
  </FlipCard>
  <FlipCard icon="🎯" title="ATT&CK is the vocabulary" accent="orange">
    Mapping findings to MITRE ATT&CK turns "the attacker did this and then this" into "T1566 → T1059.001 → T1071." That precision matters at handoff, in trend analysis, and when describing the incident to a non-technical audience.
  </FlipCard>
  <FlipCard icon="🌐" title="Intel is a hypothesis source" accent="orange">
    Threat intelligence is most useful as a hypothesis generator, not as ground truth. Uncover uses intel to guide what to look for, then validates with the evidence in the environment.
  </FlipCard>
  <FlipCard icon="🧭" title="Tools serve the question" accent="orange">
    A common Uncover failure mode is starting with a tool ("let me run a SIEM query") instead of starting with a question. The methodology asks what the analyst needs to know, then chooses the tool that can answer it.
  </FlipCard>
</div>
