## What Uncover hands forward

The evidence package Risk rates. Write it into the timeline (section 2) and evidence chain (section 4) of the <a href="/templates/handoff-packet.md" download>handoff-packet.md</a> template; at Level 1 the same items fit in a few lines of the <a href="/templates/alert-report.md" download>alert report</a>. Six items:

1. **The evidence chain:** what happened, in order, in UTC, each step with its source and confidence label, tagged with ATT&CK techniques where one applies and "none observed" where none does. ATT&CK is an unordered taxonomy, so the sequence is the analyst's contribution.
2. **Entity verdicts:** which of Scope's entities were involved, which were ruled out, and which a control contained, each with its evidence.
3. **Intelligence context:** what matched, at what confidence, and whether it was evidence or only a hypothesis source. Attribution carries its own label.
4. **Coverage gaps:** what could not be examined, and why. A gap is written as a gap, never as quiet.
5. **Detection gaps:** techniques no rule flagged, rules that should have been narrower, and the requests filed.
6. **The hypothesis ledger:** every line with its status, refuted ones included, plus the four dry-thread checks for any thread closed on empty results.

<a id="what-risk-actually-does"></a>
## What Risk does with it

Risk rates impact if real, the likelihood that the activity is malicious or unauthorized, and analytic confidence on the <a href="/docs/documentation/standards/#confidence">shared rubric</a>, then turns them into a matrix cell, a priority and a recommended move: close, escalate, or rescope and keep looking. Risk recommends; the Escalation decision makes and records the call. The <a href="/docs/risk/">Risk chapter</a> covers how.

## Depth and the close path

Uncover never closes a case. A benign chain (benign-confirmed, or authorized: a benign true positive) goes to Risk with the same rigor as a malicious one, and the documented close is recorded at the Escalation decision. A confirmed criterion is the early exit, from here as from any phase: escalate under the <a href="/docs/escalation/criteria/">escalation tripwire</a>, and Uncover continues only as IR-assigned scoping support. Level 0 never reaches Uncover. Level 1 narrows it to the one question the verdict turns on. Level 2 gets the full package.

<a id="common-uncover-to-risk-failure-modes"></a>
## Four ways the handoff fails

Missing confidence labels make the chain look more certain than it is. Silent coverage gaps let Risk recommend a close the late data reopens. Premature attribution survives only until someone questions it. An untagged narrative forces Risk to translate before it can compare. The tags make the narrative portable; they do not replace it.

## One handoff, filled in

<Reveal summary="Case A: a clean Uncover-to-Risk handoff" variant="example">

> - **<DefineTerm term="Threat" /> type:** confirmed compromise via a spearphishing link to a paste-and-run lure, with a stolen session replayed into the cloud finance platform.
> - **Chain:** delivery 09:02:11 (T1566.002) → link followed 09:10:05, command pasted into the Run dialog 09:11:40 (T1204.004) → PowerShell cradle 09:11:41 (T1059.001) → transfer 09:11:43 (T1105) → beacon (T1071.001) → cookie theft 09:19:12 (T1539) → replayed sign-in 10:41:07 (T1550.004). All times UTC.
> - **Confidence per step:** delivery and execution high (message trace and EDR, corroborated by the proxy); cookie theft high (EDR process access and file read, corroborated by the 09:19:40 upload in the proxy log); replay high (identity provider, corroborated by the platform's own log); attribution low.
> - **Primary entities (confirmed):** dlin and laptop-finance-09 (execution: high, EDR corroborated by the proxy); dlin's cloud SSO identity (replay: high, identity provider corroborated by the platform's own log); Finance user 2 and laptop-finance-12 (execution: high, EDR corroborated by separately collected PowerShell logs and a matching hash).
> - **Secondary entities:** laptop-finance-09's machine account (no unusual activity, medium); Finance user 3 on laptop-finance-14 (PowerShell blocked at launch by the EDR's script-control policy, no execution, high: contained by a control); Finance user 4 (never opened, high). Cloud finance platform: replayed session confirmed, in-app activity unknown.
> - **Escalation status:** escalation tripwire met at 09:24 UTC on criterion 1; criteria 2 and 3 confirmed at 11:05. The page has not gone out, so the first recommendation is to escalate now.
> - **Regulatory:** possible PCI exposure (compliance owns whether the platform touches card data) and candidate GDPR exposure (EU vendor-contact records) flagged in Scope; compliance and the DPO engaged at 09:45.
> - **Live status:** the replayed session was still active on the platform at 13:00. No containment has run; session revocation and platform-side termination wait on the escalation.
> - **<DefineTerm term="Intelligence" /> context:** the stage's hash (the upd.ps1 self-copy) matches one known campaign entry: one indicator, one category, attribution low. The C2 <DefineTerm term="Domain" display="domain" /> is four days old and does not match prior intel.
> - **Coverage gaps:** cloud finance platform's in-app activity is in vendor logs, request pending (a gap, not quiet). Hosts off the corporate network and VPN bypass the proxy. Mobile-app activity on BYOD phones is invisible.
> - **Detection gaps:** the rule fired on laptop-finance-09 and not on laptop-finance-12, though both ran the same payload. Cause: the rule's network leg reads only the web proxy, and laptop-finance-12 was off the corporate network without the VPN. Nothing alerted on a non-browser process reading the browser's cookie store, or on an existing session used from a hosting-provider address. Feature requests opened.
> - **Open question for Risk:** Is the current scope (5 primary + 3 secondary + 1 SaaS platform) sufficient, or should Risk push back to Scope to widen the sweep to every host that was off the proxy during the window, since the proxy-based sweep cannot see them?

One screen of structured text, every line pointing Risk toward its rating and recommendation, none requiring the SIEM query history.

</Reveal>

The benign close (Case B) uses the same format, shifted to what makes the close defensible; the escalation-tripwire case (Case C) is shorter on deliberation and longer on pending items. Both are in the [working example](/docs/uncover/example/#case-b).

## You are ready for Risk if

- Every timeline row and ledger line carries a confidence label or is recorded as a gap, and every "zero" names the query behind it.
- Risk could recommend a move from the handoff alone, without re-running a query.

<KeyTakeaway>
Uncover's deliverable is a narrative supported by evidence, with explicit confidence and explicit gaps. Done well, Risk's recommendation is obvious from the handoff alone; done poorly, Risk has to redo Uncover's work first.
</KeyTakeaway>
